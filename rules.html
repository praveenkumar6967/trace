EVENT RULES

TRACE × X // CYBER CRIME INVESTIGATION // THE TRUTH LEAVES A TRACE

All participating forensic units must adhere strictly to the following event regulations throughout the investigation challenge. Integrity monitoring is active on all workstation terminals.

01

TEAM FORMATION & CAPACITY

Each team may consist of Maximum 4 members. Participants can register under SOLO (1 member), DUO (2 members), TRIO (3 members), or SQUAD (4 members) formations.

02

MANDATORY PRE-REGISTRATION

All team members must be officially registered for the event before accessing the terminals. Upon successful registration, the system issues a unique Team ID and private Security PIN required for participant login.

03

INVESTIGATION SCOPE & DOMAINS

Challenges are rooted in Computer Science and Cybersecurity: Phishing detection, social media account hijacking, ransomware analysis, network forensics, malware dissection, and cloud breach forensics.

04

ROUND 1 TIME ALLOTMENT

Round 1 consists of 10 Forensic Questions with a strict time allotment of 10 Minutes (600 Seconds). The countdown timer is authoritative and refresh-proof.

05

ROUND 2 TIME ALLOTMENT

Qualifying teams advance to Round 2 featuring 10 Advanced Investigation Scenarios allotted 10 Minutes. All answers are automatically preserved and synchronized.

06

AUTOMATED EVALUATION CYCLE

Following completion, an automated 5-Minute Forensic Evaluation Cycle processes all responses securely. Individual scores and marks remain strictly confidential to ensure unbiased adjudication.

07

FULLSCREEN WORKSTATION MANDATE

Participants must enter and remain in Browser Fullscreen Mode throughout the investigation. Exiting fullscreen triggers an immediate integrity alert and pauses investigation workflow.

08

ANTI-CHEAT & ACTIVITY MONITORING

Tab switching, minimizing the browser, window blurring, and external clipboard copying are strictly audited. Accumulating 3 Violation Strikes results in immediate terminal lockout and disqualification.

09

SINGLE CASE DOSSIER ASSIGNMENT

Each team must inspect and choose one designated case file from the case briefing board. Once selected and opened, the assignment is locked for that team's investigation cycle.

10

CERTIFICATES & RECOGNITION

Official digital Certificate of Participation is awarded to all registered team participants. Top-scoring forensic investigative units will be honored with prestigious symposium merit citations.
